Why this matters.

Stablecoins are quietly rewriting payments, and agentic AI is rewriting how work gets done. Canada has no curriculum for the engineers, researchers, and operators who will have to ship at that intersection, so this day is where we draft it.

01
Direct access to stablecoin thought leaders
A full day in working conversation with the practitioners, researchers, and policy voices shaping how stablecoins move money in Canada and globally, so attendees leave with relationships, not slides.
Takeaway
02
Entrepreneurship pathways unique to Alberta
Alberta currently offers Canada's most accommodating regulatory and banking environment for fintech and blockchain ventures, so founders and prospective founders can explore concrete support, capital, and partnership pathways from idea to launch.
Takeaway
03
A pipeline into industry roles
The room includes banks, payment processors, AI labs, and infrastructure firms hiring at the stablecoin and agentic AI intersection, so students and early-career engineers connect directly with internship and employment opportunities.
